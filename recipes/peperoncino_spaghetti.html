Erik's Tasting Notes:
-
Peperoncino spaghetti is proof that sometimes the simplest things bring the biggest smiles. Just a swirl of golden olive oil, a whisper of garlic, and a sprinkle of dried red chili peppers, and—bam!—you’ve got pasta magic. In Italy, they call it aglio, olio e peperoncino (which sounds as fancy as it tastes), but don’t be fooled by the chili peppers, they bring more smoky charm than fiery heat. In fact, it’s so gentle even kids can twirl their forks happily. Comfort food, Italian style.
-

Ingredients

  • 250g Spaghetti
  • 450ml Water
  • ¼ cup Extra virgin olive oil
  • 4 Garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp Dried red chili peppers
  • ¼ cup Flat leaf parsley, finely chopped
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Using the inner bowl, add spaghetti, water, 2 tablespoons olive oil, garlic, and chili peppers, and stir.
  2. Close the lid and choose the [Slow Cook] function. Set the timer to 20 minutes and press Start.
  3. When the cooking is done, transfer the pasta to a serving bowl and toss with the remaining two tablespoons olive oil, parsley, salt and pepper.
  4. Serve with freshly grated parmesan cheese.
